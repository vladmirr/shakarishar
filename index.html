ABOUT SHAKARI SHAR

Pioneering Industrial Supply & Engineering Excellence

Shakari Shar is a specialized industrial supplier operating at the forefront of the mining and iron & steel sectors. Based in Sulaymaniyah with an active international supply network, we analyze on-site industrial demands with rigorous discipline, precision engineering, and a corporate dedication to excellence.

Our core mission is to empower mining operations and heavy metallurgical plants by delivering high-performance machinery, certified wear parts, specialized consumables, and turnkey technical support.

Built on principles of integrity, reliability, and technical competence, Shakari Shar is the trusted bridge between world-class manufacturers and heavy industrial enterprises across Iraq and the wider region.

CATALOGUE OVERVIEW

Key Product & Supply Categories

High-grade industrial components engineered to withstand extreme mechanical stress and thermal fatigue.

01

Crushing & Screening

Jaw plates, cone mantles, concaves, screen meshes, and heavy impact blow bars.

02

Milling & Grinding

Forged and cast steel grinding balls, high-chromium mill liners, and trunnion bearings.

03

Conveying & Bulk Handling

Heavy heat-resistant rubber belts, rollers, idlers, pulleys, and automated belt scrapers.

04

Raw Materials & Metallurgy

Pumps & Fluid Handling

Lab & Testing Gear
